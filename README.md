## Evidence of Insurability (EOI) 

EOI is the process used to determine whether a person qualifies certain Financial Protection insurance products. 

It's a medical underwriting process required for certain FP products (like critical illness, supplemental health) 

An underwriter reviews each application case-by-case to verify the applicant truly has the claimed condition and doesn't have excluded conditions 

*Purpose:* Prevent adverse selection and ensure the applicant qualifies before coverage is approved, and future claims can be paid. 

FP products are a guaranteed issue. For high-risk products (critical illness, certain supplemental coverages), UHC must verify health status before approving the application.
